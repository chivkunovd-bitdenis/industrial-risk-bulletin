Zelvanor Petrochem Terminal: fire at tank farm, regulator fine and supplier lawsuit

Industrial Risk Bulletin · 4 November 2026

Zelvanor Petrochem Terminal JSC, the operator of a crude oil and bitumen storage terminal on the Kaspiysk industrial coast, has had a difficult year. Public records and statements reviewed for this bulletin point to a serious fire, an environmental penalty and a commercial dispute with the terminal's only crude supplier.

Tank farm fire

On 14 March 2026 a fire broke out at tank No. 7 of the Zelvanor tank farm, a 20,000 cubic metre crude oil tank. The terminal was shut down for 41 days. According to the company's own statement, the direct damage to the tank and the adjacent pipe rack was estimated at USD 18.5 million, and throughput for the first half of 2026 fell by 37 percent compared with the same period of 2025.

Environmental fine

In June 2026 the regional environmental inspectorate fined Zelvanor Petrochem Terminal JSC RUB 42 million for soil and groundwater contamination around tank No. 7 after the fire, and ordered remediation of the site by the end of 2027.

Dispute with the sole crude supplier

In September 2026 Northline Crude Trading, which supplies all of the crude oil handled by the terminal, filed an arbitration claim against Zelvanor for USD 7.2 million in unpaid storage shortfall charges. Zelvanor depends on this one supplier and on a single jetty for all marine loading; the jetty was out of service for nine days during the March fire.

What it means for the risk

The terminal combines a recent large fire loss, an open environmental remediation order and a single-supplier, single-jetty business model. Any repeat event at the jetty would stop the terminal's revenue completely.
